04 / Research

Understanding how shells behave.

My doctoral research explores static and free-vibration response in composite, sandwich, and functionally graded shell panels.

Ph.D. / Savitribai Phule Pune University / 2025

Four-node flat shell finite elements

I developed and validated finite element formulations for moderately thick cylindrical, spherical, and conical shell panels. The work spans shell mechanics, layered materials, boundary conditions, stiffness and mass matrices, and benchmark validation.

About me

A designer's experience.
A researcher's curiosity.

I am a structural design engineer with more than a decade of professional design experience and over five years of academic teaching experience. My work brings together reinforced concrete, steel, formwork systems, and advanced finite element research.

As the founder of Vardhaman Engineers and Consultants, I worked with architects, developers, manufacturers, and construction teams to turn structural design into practical solutions. My Ph.D. deepened that practice through the study of composite and functionally graded shell structures.

Today, I am based in Eden Prairie and interested in structural engineering, advanced analysis, and design opportunities in the Minneapolis-Saint Paul area.
