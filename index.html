02 / Research

Looking beneath
the surface.

My doctoral work asks how complex shell structures respond, and how finite element models can describe that behavior reliably.

CylindricalSphericalConical

Ph.D. in Structural Engineering / 2025

Small elements.
A deeper understanding.

I developed and validated four-node flat shell finite elements for static and free-vibration analysis of composite, sandwich, and functionally graded shell panels.

In my 2022 work with Shripad Kulkarni, the DKZigTS1 formulation uses seven degrees of freedom per node. We compared deflections, stresses, natural frequencies, and mode shapes with analytical benchmarks and three-dimensional ANSYS models.

03 / About me

Built on experience.
Driven by curiosity.

I am a structural engineer who enjoys the conversation between good design, rigorous analysis, and the people who make a project happen.

My professional path spans more than a decade of structural design, over five years of academic teaching, and doctoral research in shell finite elements. As the founder of Vardhaman Engineers and Consultants, I worked with architects, developers, manufacturers, and site teams across reinforced concrete, steel, formwork, and municipal infrastructure.

I am now based in Eden Prairie, Minnesota, and interested in structural design and advanced analysis opportunities in the Minneapolis-Saint Paul area.
